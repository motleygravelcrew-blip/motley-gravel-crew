# Motley Gravel Crew — Website Visual Style Guide

## Purpose
This guide defines the approved visual direction for the MGC website. It is an aesthetic system only. Existing page purposes, wording, content, destinations, links, forms, embeds and interactions are to remain intact.

## Brand assets
- Use the approved MGC transparent emblem at `assets/mgc-logo-transparent.png` in the site header and footer.
- Keep the full-colour logo artwork intact. Do not redraw, recolour, distort, crop, glow, bevel or add effects to the logo.
- Retain the existing two-rider hero artwork at `assets/mgc-hero-banner.jpg`: a gravel/road cyclist and a mountain-bike rider. Do not replace it with a different rider image or a text-only banner.

## Colour palette
| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight navy | Primary dark background | `#0B1226` |
| Off black | Deep header/footer | `#050A16` |
| Cyan | Primary accent | `#00E5FF` |
| Electric blue | Secondary accent | `#007BFF` |
| Violet | Accent | `#8A00FF` |
| Magenta | Accent | `#FF00D6` |
| White | Main light surface | `#FFFFFF` |
| Light grey | Supporting surface | `#E9EEF5` |
| Mid grey | Secondary text | `#9AA4B2` |

Use navy and white as the structural colours. The cyan-to-blue-to-violet-to-magenta gradient is an accent, not a background for every component.

## Typography
- Headlines and navigation: Barlow Condensed, bold or extra-bold; uppercase where appropriate.
- Body copy and forms: Inter, regular/medium.
- Maintain strong hierarchy, readable line lengths and clear contrast.

## Layout and components
- Header: deep navy, approved transparent logo and existing wordmark, compact horizontal navigation, clear active-page accent.
- Hero: full-width existing two-rider banner; keep it crisp and legible, with no excessive glow or decorative overlays.
- Discipline navigation: a full-width horizontal strip with ROAD, GRAVEL, XCO, XCM, CYCLOCROSS, BIKEPACKING and COMMUNITY, separated by small violet dots. Use a white/light strip on the homepage as in the approved reference.
- Homepage: clear introduction and calls to action, then a three-card feature row for Cycling Intelligence, Route Library and Community; a smaller quick-link row for Events, Cycling Links and About; existing newsletter and Strava content remains.
- Inner pages: consistent light content surface, strong navy page headings, cyan/gradient accents, tidy cards and readable content widths.
- Footer: deep navy with existing links and brand lock-up.

## Style principles
- Clean, crisp, modern and purposeful.
- Use white space to separate sections.
- Prefer simple borders and restrained shadows.
- Use diagonal cyan/blue/violet/magenta stripe motifs sparingly, referencing the jersey.
- Avoid neon glow, excessive gradients, noisy textures, unnecessary boxes and decorative clutter.
- Mobile layouts must remain readable and usable.

## Non-negotiable scope limits
- Do not change existing content, page purpose, wording, links, URL destinations, forms, embeds, scripts, navigation destinations or functionality.
- Do not introduce Team or rider-development messaging at this stage.
- Do not redesign or alter the approved logo or hero artwork.
