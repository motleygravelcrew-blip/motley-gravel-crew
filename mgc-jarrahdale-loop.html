ABOUT THIS ROUTE

A 25.3 km mountain-bike loop through the beautiful jarrah forests of Jarrahdale, incorporating the Langford Park trail network. With 407 m of climbing, the route offers a rewarding mix of rolling terrain, repeated climbs, flowing descents and plenty of forest riding.

A great intermediate-level MTB adventure that balances fitness, fun and technical riding, making it an ideal weekend outing for the Motley Gravel Crew.
