THE INTELLIGENCE EDITION

Thursday, 26 November 2026 · Perth, Western Australia

Thursday, 26 November 2026 | Perth, Western Australia

THE MGC READ
Today’s edition adds several important developments for Australian and Western Australian riders: Jay Vine has committed to UAE Team Emirates-XRG through 2029; the 2027 AusCycling Gravel National Championships are confirmed for Nabawa in the Mid West; and AusCycling’s 2027 esports programme includes a national championship final in Brisbane. We also cover Egan Bernal’s new deal, the opening weekend of the UCI Cyclo-cross World Cup, Perth’s 2027 BMX Nationals, Connor Wright’s latest national honour and RoadNats Gran Fondo entry status.

01 — ROAD

Jay Vine extends UAE Team Emirates-XRG contract through 2029

27 October: Jay Vine has committed his future to UAE Team Emirates-XRG through the end of the 2029 season. The Australian joined the team in 2023 and has established himself as a versatile climber and time-trial specialist, combining individual opportunities with an important support role across the squad.

Vine’s recent results include two overall victories at the Tour Down Under, Australian national time-trial titles and multiple Vuelta a España stage wins. He finished his 2026 campaign with second place at the Chrono des Nations, behind Remco Evenepoel.

Why it matters: the extension gives one of Australia’s leading WorldTour riders long-term stability with a team that has supported his development. Vine says he is already building towards the 2027 season.

Sources: UAE Team Emirates-XRG — official contract announcement, 27 October · Cyclingnews — contract analysis

Egan Bernal commits to Netcompany INEOS

24 November: Netcompany INEOS confirmed that Egan Bernal has signed a new contract, extending a relationship that began in 2018. The Colombian won the 2019 Tour de France and 2021 Giro d’Italia before a serious training crash in 2022. He returned to race both the Giro and Tour in 2026.

The team has not published the length of the new agreement. Bernal said he was looking forward to building towards next season and helping the group achieve something special.

Why it matters: Bernal’s future had been a talking point after a difficult period in his career. The new deal gives the team continuity around a rider with Grand Tour-winning experience, while allowing Bernal to continue his comeback within a familiar programme.

Sources: Netcompany INEOS — official announcement, 24 November · Reuters — contract report

02 — CYCLOCROSS

UCI World Cup opens with a Czech double-header

The 2026–27 UCI Cyclo-cross World Cup begins in Czechia this weekend, with Ostrava on Friday 27 November followed by Tábor on Sunday 29 November. The 12-round series runs through to Hoogerheide on 24 January.

Ostrava brings a night-racing format: the elite women race at 18:15 local time on Friday, followed by the elite men at 19:45. The venue programme also includes junior races on Thursday evening.

Mathieu van der Poel and Lucinda Brand enter the season as the reigning World Cup overall champions and current cyclocross world champions. The opening rounds will provide the first early indication of form across a long winter campaign.

Why it matters: the World Cup is the sport’s leading international series. The opening double-header gives the specialists an immediate two-race test and starts the build towards the Christmas and New Year block.

Sources: UCI — 2026–27 World Cup preview, 24 November · Flanders Classics — full calendar · Ostrava — event schedule

03 — XCO / XCM

Connor Wright named AusCycling’s Junior MTB Male Athlete of the Year

Western Australian Connor Wright has been named Junior Mountain Bike Male Athlete of the Year at the 2026 AusCycling Awards — the second consecutive year he has received the honour.

The Burekup rider’s standout season included winning the junior men’s cross-country Olympic world title at the UCI Mountain Bike World Championships. AusCycling also highlighted four consecutive UCI XCO Junior Series victories, the under-19 Oceania XCO title and the under-19 national XCC and XCO titles.

Why it matters: Wright’s results show the strength of the WA off-road pathway and the level of consistency required to convert junior promise into international results. His progress across mountain biking and road racing is one to keep watching.

Source: AusCycling — 2026 Awards and winners · Australian Cycling Team — Wright’s junior XCO world title

04 — WESTERN AUSTRALIA

Perth confirmed as host of the 2027 BMX Racing National Championships

AusCycling has confirmed that the 2027 BMX Racing National Championships will be held at Westside BMX in Perth from 15–18 April. The announcement completes the national BMX racing calendar for 2027, which is scheduled to feature at least 55 competition days at state and national level.

The championships will cover 20-inch and Cruiser categories, from Mini Wheelers and Sprockets through to Challenge, Masters, Para BMX and championship classes. Westside BMX previously hosted the national championships in 2024.

Why it matters: another national-level cycling championship is coming to WA, creating a major home-state opportunity for local riders and clubs, and a significant event for the Perth BMX community.

Sources: AusCycling / BMX Nationals — venue announcement, 25 November · 2027 BMX Nationals — event information

RoadNats Perth: long-course Gran Fondo allocation exhausted

The entry allocation for the 81.6km 2027 Gran Fondo National Championships on Sunday 10 January has been exhausted. The event organiser says additional places may be released after eligibility checks and has a waitlist for riders who want to be notified.

The 27.2km Gran Fondo Short Course remains a separate option, open to members and non-members aged 15 or older. It uses two laps of the 13.6km closed-road circuit through Kings Park, beside the Swan River and into the Perth CBD.

Rider note: the long course is currently at capacity; the short course is the available alternative for riders wanting to take part in the Perth event.

Sources: Road Nationals — National Championships entry status · Road Nationals — Short Course information

05 — GRAVEL

Gravel National Championships confirmed for Nabawa and Chapman Valley

7 August 2027: AusCycling has confirmed that the 2027 Gravel National Championships will be held in Nabawa and the Chapman Valley, with Backroads Gravel hosting the event. The championships will bring elite, masters and junior riders to the Mid West to compete across the region’s gravel-road network.

The event marks the first time the national gravel championships have been held in Western Australia’s Mid West. AusCycling expects leading elite and age-group riders from around the country to contest the national titles.

Why it matters: this is a major opportunity for WA gravel riding. It puts the Chapman Valley and Nabawa on the national cycling calendar and gives the region a platform to showcase its roads, landscapes and local communities.

Sources: AusCycling — 2027 Gravel National Championships · AusCycling — event announcement, 29 September · WA Government — Mid West championships announcement

06 — TECHNOLOGY

AusCycling confirms key dates for 2027 esports national championships

AusCycling’s 2027 esports programme includes the QUT Esports National Championships in Brisbane on Friday 7 May 2027. Riders will first contest an online MyWhoosh qualifier on Tuesday 27 April, with five men and five women earning places in the live final at Queensland University of Technology’s The Cube.

The wider programme also includes the 2027 Masters Esports National Championships, scheduled to take place online on MyWhoosh on 20 July. AusCycling membership is required to compete, with further eligibility conditions applying to the Masters event.

Why it matters: the programme gives Australian riders another national pathway outside traditional road and off-road racing, combining online qualification with a live national championship final.

Sources: AusCycling — 2027 QUT Esports National Championships · AusCycling — online qualifier details · AusCycling — Masters Esports National Championships
