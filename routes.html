HAVE A ROUTE TO SHARE?

Email your public Strava route link to motleygravelcrew@gmail.com. Include the route name, location, riding type, distance, elevation gain and a short note about what makes it worth riding. If you would like contributor credit, include the name or riding name you want displayed. Contributor credit is entirely optional; routes can be published without your name.

Please make sure your Strava route is shareable by link. Do not include private home addresses or personal information in the route description.
